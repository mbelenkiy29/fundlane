# Fundlane website and demo privacy notice — approved September 11, 2026

Operator: **Sentinel Tech Solutions LLC**
Privacy contact: **ben@sentineltechsolutions.io**
Effective date: **September 11, 2026**

This notice covers Fundlane’s public marketing website and demo requests. It does not describe how a brokerage handles merchant information inside its Fundlane workspace.

## Information we collect

When you request a demo, we collect your name, work email, brokerage name, team size, and any message you choose to provide. Please do not include merchant records, bank details, government identifiers, or other sensitive information in your message.

Our hosting and security services also process technical information needed to serve and protect the website, such as your IP address, browser information, request time, and requested page. The demo endpoint uses request-rate limits and an opaque request identifier to prevent abuse and duplicate submissions.

## How we use information

We use demo details to review your inquiry, arrange a walkthrough, and respond to your questions about Fundlane. We use technical information to operate the website, diagnose problems, and prevent abuse. Submitting a demo request does not subscribe you to automated email or text campaigns.

## Access and service providers

Authorized Fundlane operators can review demo requests. Render hosts the website and receiver, and Neon stores encrypted demo records. Authentication services are provided by Clerk when you use account-related features. These providers process information needed to provide their services. We may disclose information when required by law or necessary to protect the service and its users.

The demo flow does not send your inquiry to lenders, sell your contact details, or use your submission for targeted advertising. No advertising analytics have been added to the demo form. Account features may use cookies or similar storage needed for authentication.

## Storage and deletion

Demo contact details are encrypted before they are stored. Access to the private inbox is limited to operators with deployment and database access. We keep inquiries while needed to handle them and maintain relevant business records, then delete them when no longer needed. Copies in provider backups may remain until those backups expire. No system can guarantee absolute security.

## Your choices

You may choose not to submit a request. Contact **ben@sentineltechsolutions.io** to ask about your information, request a correction or deletion, or ask us to stop following up. We may need to verify your request and may retain information where required by law. Rights available to you depend on your location.

## Audience and changes

Fundlane’s demo service is intended for business users, not children. We may update this notice as the service changes and will show the effective date above.
